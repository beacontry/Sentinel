Tiers vs. roles — the two axes

Beacontry uses two orthogonal access systems. They never conflict; sometimes they stack.

Tier answers "what features can you use?" Hierarchical: Premium > Trader > Free.

Role answers "are you running the platform?" Binary: admin or user.

An admin can be on the Free tier (they paid for nothing). A regular user can be on Premium (they pay for everything). Admin can grant any tier manually from /dashboard/admin; in production, Stripe Checkout grants paid tiers automatically.

How upgrades, downgrades, and cancellations work

Upgrade mid-cycle (e.g., Trader → Premium)

Stripe prorates immediately. You're refunded the unused portion of Trader and charged the prorated cost of Premium for the rest of the period. Tier flips within ~3 seconds of payment confirmation via our webhook. Audit log records the change with USER_TIER_CHANGED.

Downgrade (e.g., Premium → Trader)

Same mechanics, in reverse. Stripe prorates a credit applied to your next invoice. Your tier flips immediately to the lower one; you keep your data (watchlists, journal, broker connections) — only paid feature access changes.

Cadence switch (Monthly ↔ Annual)

Stripe prorates. Switching to Annual mid-month gives you a credit for the remaining month + charges the annual rate; the annual cycle starts from that day.

Cancellation (default: at period end)

Click Manage subscription in /dashboard/billing to open the Stripe Customer Portal. Pick Cancel subscription — defaults to ending at the next renewal so you keep paid access for what you already paid for. At period end, your tier reverts to Free automatically via webhook. Data is preserved; resubscribing picks up where you left off.

Refunds — first 30 days

Full refund on request, no questions asked. Email hello@beacontry.com or open a support ticket from your dashboard. Refunds process to the original payment method within 5–10 business days via Stripe.

Payment failures

If your card declines on renewal, Stripe retries for ~5 days and emails you. Your tier stays active during the dunning window. If retries all fail, the subscription cancels and tier reverts to Free.

How tier gates work (technical)

Two enforcement layers, both required:

Server (the security boundary)

Every paid API route calls await checkTier(userId, "trader" | "premium") from src/lib/tiers-server.ts immediately after the auth check. Free users hitting paid routes get a 402 TIER_INSUFFICIENT response. This is what actually protects the feature — bypassing it requires a database-level tier mutation, which only admins can perform.

Client (the UX layer)

The same paid pages render a <PaywallBanner> at the top via useTier() hook + userHasTier() check. Free users see the upgrade prompt instead of confused empty states. The page itself still renders; actions fail with the 402 envelope, and the UI explains why.

Engine tier-awareness

When the engine starts, it captures engine.userTier from the database. The hybrid signal pipeline strips Premium-tier layers (AI scoring) for non-Premium users — Trader-tier users get the Finnhub-driven layers (sentiment, options, analyst) but skip the Groq-driven AI scoring layer they don't pay for. Mid-session tier changes don't reshape a running engine; they take effect on the next restart.

Source of truth

Stripe is the source of truth for subscription state. We mirror only users.tier + users.tier_expires_at + users.stripe_customer_id in our database. Subscription lifecycle events (creation, plan switches, cancellations, payment failures) flow through our webhook at /api/webhooks/stripe, which updates the mirror.

Common pricing questions

Why is Premium ($40) more than 2× Trader ($20)?

It isn't — Premium is $40/mo, Trader is $20/mo, so Premium is exactly 2× Trader. The AI features (Groq inference + storage + processing) cost meaningfully more than the Finnhub-only Trader plan, so the premium reflects real marginal cost on our side.

Can I skip Trader and go straight to Premium?

Yes. New users can sign up for either plan directly from /pricing. Both include the 7-day free trial.

What if I'm on the free trial and decide to cancel?

Cancel anytime during the trial in the Stripe Customer Portal — you won't be charged. The trial ends immediately on cancellation (not at the original trial end date).

If I cancel after the trial, do I keep my data?

Yes. Your account, watchlists, journal entries, trade history, broker connections, and audit log all persist. Only paid feature access is removed at the end of the billing period. Resubscribing later picks up exactly where you left off.

Do you offer discounts for students / educators / open-source contributors?

Yes — email hello@beacontry.com with a verifiable .edu address or a GitHub link to your OSS contributions. We'll comp a Trader tier for the duration of your enrollment or active contribution period.

Why is there a 7-day trial and not 14 or 30 days?

Long trials hurt commitment more than they help conversion — users who haven't engaged in 7 days won't engage in 14. 7 days is enough to run the engine on paper across multiple market sessions and see signals fire. If you need more time, email us; we'll extend on request.
