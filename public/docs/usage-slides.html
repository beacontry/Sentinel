Eight engine modes

Different risk postures, different scan cadences. Pick one, or pick adaptive and let the regime classifier choose.

Conservative

Lower confidence threshold, wider stops, fewer concurrent positions.

Moderate

Balanced. The default if you're not sure where to start.

Optimized

Uses GA-tuned parameters from the optimizer. Updated when you accept a new tune.

Aggressive

Tighter stops, higher position size, looser confidence gate. Risk-on environments only.

Intraday

5-min bars, 1-min scans. PDT-sensitive under $25k.

Tactical

SPY trend filter — enters when above SMA, exits when below. Bull-only.

Tactical Smart

Tactical + screener-scored entries. Always-on rebalance to top signals.

Adaptive

Reads VIX + SPY + breadth, picks the right base mode for the regime.

Concepts · 3 of 7

Risk profile is the single source of truth

Set it once on the Trader page. Every engine, every backtest, every order respects these limits.

Account size

Base for position sizing. The engine never sizes a position above maxPositionPct of this.

Max daily loss %

Engine auto-halts when daily losses exceed this. Resumes next trading day.

Max position %

Size per trade as fraction of equity. Also caps concurrent position count (100 / pct).

Max consecutive losses

Auto-halt after N losses in a row. Resets on any winner.

Sector exposure cap

Reject new BUYs that would push a sector above this fraction of equity.

Earnings blackout

Skip BUYs on symbols with earnings in the next N days.

Concepts · 4 of 7

The broker is the source of truth

Positions, fills, cash — Beacontry reads them live from your broker every scan. The database is a cache, not the record.

  • Multiple brokers supported: Alpaca (paper + live), Tradier (paper + live), IBKR (live only). Add your keys in Settings.
  • Paper trades are real Alpaca paper trades — they go to Alpaca's paper engine, get matched against live market data, fills come back through the same code path live uses. Faithful test of signal quality and risk profile.
  • Live trading requires explicit unlock: set ALLOW_LIVE_TRADING=1 in the server env. Without it the engine refuses to start on any live broker. Five capital circuit breakers fire automatically on every BUY (notional cap, rate limit, broker failure, account switch, consecutive losses).
  • You can switch brokers without restart from the sidebar. Engine reloads with the new active connection on the next scan.

Concepts · 6 of 7

Tamper-evident audit log

Every privileged action writes a hash-chained row. You can verify the chain with one click.

  • What gets logged: logins, order placements, halts, config changes, role changes, system-config rotations, MFA setup, watchlist sharing, broker connection changes.
  • How the chain works: each row stores a SHA-256 of (prev_hash, action, actor, resource, metadata, timestamp). Genesis row has prev_hash = "GENESIS". Tampering with any row breaks every subsequent row's hash.
  • Verify chain button on /dashboard/admin/audit walks the entire log and reports any break, with the specific row.
  • What's safe to truncate: the whole log via TRUNCATE audit_log resets to a new genesis. Partial deletes break the chain permanently.

Pages · Trading

Backtest, Compare, Optimize

Three tools, one workflow: prove the strategy in historical data before you live-trade it.

Backtest

Run any strategy preset (or custom) against any symbol + date range. Equity curve, win rate, max drawdown, Sharpe + Sortino + Calmar + MAR.

Compare

Pick up to 5 saved strategies. Side-by-side stats columns + equity-curve overlay normalized to start = 100. Mode-compare runs all 8 modes against one symbol.

Optimizer

Genetic algorithm tunes signal parameters (EMA/RSI/stops/TP) on a training window, validates on hold-out. Top tune deployable as "optimized" mode.

Plus

  • Replay — step through historical trades bar-by-bar with markers on the chart. The "why did I do that?" tool.
  • Risk Simulator — what happens to your portfolio if SPY drops 10%? 20%? Stress-test your sizing.
  • Manual Order Ticket — /dashboard/trade/{SYM}. Market / limit / stop / bracket. Engine-gated (refused while engine is running, to prevent state drift).
Pages · Journal · 1 of 3

Journal v2 — auto-stubs and prompts

The blank-canvas problem solved. Beacontry writes the trade mechanics; you write the WHY.

Auto-stubs on every fill

  • Trade closes → journal entry created automatically
  • Pre-fills symbol, P&L, signal, fill price, qty
  • Adds leading questions: "What did I get right or wrong about this trade?" / "What's the lesson?"
  • Idempotent: re-running reconciliation never creates duplicates

Daily prompts

  • Pre-market at 8:30 ET — 4 questions about today's plan
  • Post-market at 4:30 ET — 4 reflection questions about what worked / didn't
  • Weekends skipped. One prompt per type per day per user.
  • Unfilled prompts sort to the top of the entry list with a "Needs review" badge.
Pages · Journal · 2 of 3

Tagging that maps to behavior

30 tags across 4 categories. Each entry can have any combination.

Emotion 8

Discipline, patience, confidence, fear, greed, FOMO, revenge, boredom.

Strategy 8

Breakout, mean reversion, trend, swing, intraday, news, earnings, technical.

Execution 8

Followed plan, perfect, early exit, late entry, size too big/small, stop too tight/wide.

Outcome 6

Win, loss, breakeven, stopped out, full target, partial fill.

Why categorized?

So phase 6 (behavioral pattern badges) can answer: "Every time you tag FOMO, you lose." Per-tag win rate computed from linked trades, surfaced as a quiet badge on the journal home when there's enough data (n ≥ 5).

What's in it
