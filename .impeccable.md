## Design Context

### Users
Active traders and market analysts who use Sentinel during focused trading sessions. They are data-literate, time-sensitive, and working in dimly-lit environments (home office, trading desks). The primary job is to synthesize market intelligence quickly — scanning signals, reviewing positions, and making informed trading decisions. They expect density and precision over decoration.

### Brand Personality
**Precise, watchful, dense.** Sentinel is a tactical intelligence system, not a consumer app. It should feel like a precision instrument — like the readout of a high-end analytical tool. No flash, no personality for its own sake. Every element earns its place by communicating information.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual tone**: Dark, dense, functional. Think Bloomberg Terminal reimagined for the modern web — not a SaaS dashboard.
- **References**: Bloomberg Terminal (density), Linear (polish), Raycast (command palette UX), Arc browser (restraint)
- **Anti-references**: Generic shadcn dashboards, purple-gradient AI tools, Vercel marketing pages, anything with cards-in-cards or decorative sparklines
- **Theme**: Dark mode only. Traders work in low-light conditions during extended sessions. Emerald accent (conviction, growth, capital).
- **Color system**: OKLCH with emerald-tinted neutrals (hue 165). No pure grays.

### Design Principles
1. **Information density over decoration** — Every pixel communicates. No decorative elements that don't serve comprehension.
2. **Precision over personality** — Typography, spacing, and color serve hierarchy, not mood. Use `font-mono` for all financial data.
3. **Restraint is the aesthetic** — The design is distinctive by what it doesn't do: no gradients, no glassmorphism, no oversized radii, no side-stripe accents.
4. **Consistent visual language** — One font family (Geist), one radius scale (Tailwind defaults), one elevation system (OKLCH lightness steps).
5. **Functional motion only** — Animations communicate state changes (enter, exit, loading). No decorative motion.
